Live soccer goal lines

A calm checklist before you bet Under or Over.

SPORTHUB checks a live match against your own rules and tells you one thing: BET or NO BET. It counts your bets for the day, draws the xG, and can alert your lock screen when a match passes. It never places a bet.

Your limits

Every number is yours to change: averages, minute window, goals, shots on target and xG. Each line keeps its own limits.

Example numbers shown. The limits in the panel are starting points, not advice. Change them to suit how you trade.

How it helps

Rules, limits and a record, in one place.

Session limits

Set how many bets a day and how many days. The panel counts them down, adds a cool-off after a loss and says when you are done for the day.

Live numbers

Paste a stats line, or add your own API-Sports key and tap Live matches. It fills the minute, score, shots on target, xG and last-5 averages for you.

xG and momentum

An xG graph and a momentum view show how the match has built up, with your limit and entry window drawn on top.

Log it in one tap

Place the bet with your bookmaker, then tap Log. The panel settles it from the final score and keeps the result in your Record.

Backtest

Replay your rules over finished matches, one rule at a time, to see which ones are earning their place.

Yours alone

No account. Your settings and record stay in your own browser, and your API key never goes anywhere but API-Sports and your own alert server.

Lock-screen alerts

Close the app. It still taps you on the shoulder.

Add a tiny alert server of your own (a free Cloudflare Worker). It watches live matches with your key, runs the same rules, and pushes one alert per match that passes.

Setup steps (about 10 minutes)

On a bigger screen

The same panel, laid out for a desktop.

On a laptop or monitor the inputs sit on the left and the decision on the right, so you can watch the verdict without scrolling. On a phone it stays one tidy column.

Get it

Open it, or put it on your Home Screen.

iPhone

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open SPORTHUB from the new icon. It opens the panel full screen.
Computer

Just open the panel

  1. Click Open the panel. Bookmark it.
  2. Optional: install it from the address bar in Chrome or Edge so it opens in its own window.
  3. To get alerts, add your alert server in Settings.

Each Home Screen icon and each browser keeps its own data. Before you switch devices, tap Copy backup in Settings and paste it into the new one. The backup holds your session, rules and record, never your keys or passwords.
